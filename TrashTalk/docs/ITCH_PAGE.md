# Trash Talk: itch.io page (ready to paste)

## Title
Trash Talk

## Short description / tagline (itch "Short description" field)
Fly a junk rocket, bomb trash into talking bins, and get roasted when you miss.

## Description

**The trash cans in this town have mouths. And opinions.**

Load a rickety junk rocket with garbage, pull back and launch it into the sky, then drop bottles, soda cans, banana peels and fishbones into the waiting trash cans. Every piece keeps the rocket's momentum, so you have to lead your shots and let each one arc in.

The cans are characters, and they talk trash. Hit the bin and they chomp it down ("Bin-go!"). Miss and they laugh at you ("Ha! Litterbug!"). The snobby blue recycler refuses your banana ("Banana? How DARE you."), the hippie compost bin only wants peels, the sleepy one naps with its lid shut, the nervous one panics when your rocket flies close, and the loud dumpster eats anything you throw at it.

10 hand-made levels with walls, rooftop bins, fans that blow trash around and moving targets, ending in a boss fight with the MEGA DUMPSTER. You get 3 rockets per level. Clear it with your first rocket for 3 stars. Run out of fuel or crash and your rocket sprays its leftover junk everywhere.

## Jam: Mini Jame Gam #60

- **Theme: Trash.** Everything is trash: you deliver garbage into bins, the bins talk trash, and littering puts the piece back in your queue. Recycling vs compost sorting is part of the puzzle.
- **Special object: Rocket.** The rocket is your only tool. You slingshot-launch it, it burns fuel on its own, you steer it in flight, and it bombs trash from the air. When the fuel runs out it falls, and if it hits something it explodes.
