# Trash Talk

My entry for **Mini Jame Gam #60** (theme: Trash, special object: Rocket).

You've got a junk rocket full of garbage and a street full of trash cans that won't stop talking trash. Pull back to launch, tap to drop the trash, and get it in the right bin before you run out of rockets. Blue bins only take recycling, green ones only take food, and the Mega Dumpster at the end is not a fan of you.

## Controls

- Pull back from the rocket and let go to launch
- Tap / click or Space to drop trash
- Hold the mouse (or A/D) to steer
- R to restart, Esc to pause

## Running it

Open the folder in **Unity 6000.6.0f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/Game.unity` and hit Play. Each level is its own scene (`Level01` to `Level10`) if you want to mess with them.

Web build: `Trash Talk > Build WebGL` from the menu bar.
